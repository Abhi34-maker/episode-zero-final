    About this prototype

    The problem. A new writer often has a spark but does not know the genre, how to structure a long story, how to build a hero or how to open episode one. Many stop before they draft anything.

    How it works. You choose from ready-made answers. The concept, outline, loose ends, episode-one notes and roadmap are assembled from advice written in advance. There is no AI running in this page, so the same choices always give the same result.

    Hypothesis. Writers who reach a clear concept in one sitting are more likely to draft episode one within seven days. The measure would be the share of new writers who go from idea to first-episode draft in seven days. This has not been tested with real writers.

    Limits. The advice is general craft guidance, not a judgement of your story. Your notes stay on this page and are not saved, so copy your brief before you leave.
